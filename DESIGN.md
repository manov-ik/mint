## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in high-end editorial minimalism with a modern, technical edge. It aims to evoke a sense of focused discipline, quiet luxury, and intellectual clarity. By utilizing a "Pitch Black" environment, the interface recedes to let the content—the user's objectives—take center stage. The aesthetic balances systematic precision with a high-performance dark mode.

The target audience consists of high-performers and individuals seeking a structured, distraction-free environment for personal growth. The visual response should be one of calm, seriousness, and premium quality, reminiscent of a boutique digital journal or a bespoke productivity tool.

## Colors

The palette is monochromatic with a singular, vibrant cool-toned accent.

- **Background:** A true pitch black (#000000) for maximum contrast and focus.
- **Primary (Text):** An off-white/beige (#D9D9D9) to reduce eye strain while maintaining legibility.
- **Secondary (Accent):** A modern lavender (#a47dd6) reserved for progress indicators, highlights, and active states.
- **Tertiary (Surfaces):** A deep charcoal (#1A1A1A) for container backgrounds to provide subtle depth against the pitch-black canvas.
- **Borders:** A very low-opacity variation of the primary text color to create delicate "ghost" outlines.
